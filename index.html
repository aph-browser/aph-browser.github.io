Aph

A Firefox based browser built around workspaces.

Native workspace management, a command palette, tab stashing, and hardened privacy defaults, injected into stock Firefox. No fork. No recompilation.

Features

  • Workspaces 1–9 — Alt+1..9 to jump, Alt+Shift+]/[ to cycle, Alt+Shift+Tab for last-used. Window-scoped: every window has its own set, and the same number may show in two windows at once.
  • Command palette — workspace actions, tab/bookmark/history/stash search.
  • Stash — park tabs away with workspace + container restore, snapshot whole workspaces, and auto-sweep.
  • Tab groups — native tab groups inside workspaces.
  • Container bindings — bind a workspace to a Firefox container; new tabs open there.
  • Split view — Ctrl+Alt+\ side-by-side panes with the most-recently-viewed same-workspace tab.
  • Focus mode — Ctrl+Alt+F hides every chrome surface; session-only, per-window.
  • Workspace accents — per-workspace hue overrides alongside names, tags, and icons.
  • Privacy defaults — telemetry, health reports, Normandy, and activity-stream telemetry off. Your edits persist (seed-once prefs).
  • Welcome tour & settings — a first-run tour of workspaces, palette and tabs, plus an Aph Settings page. Both re-openable from the Aph menu and the palette.
